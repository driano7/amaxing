## A New Nonstop to the Gulf of California

Alaska Airlines has launched a direct nonstop service between Los Angeles (LAX) and La Paz, the laid-back capital of Baja California Sur. The new route shortens travel time and removes the connection hassle, opening a more convenient gateway to one of Mexico's most underrated coastal destinations.

## Why La Paz Belongs on Your Radar

La Paz sits on the calm waters of the Sea of Cortez, famous for its turquoise bays, whale watching between January and March, and easy day trips to Isla Espiritu Santo. Unlike busier resort towns, it keeps a slow, authentic pace with a malecón boardwalk, casual seafood spots and quiet beaches just minutes from downtown.

## An Easier Access Point

For travelers based in Southern California, the nonstop flight makes a long weekend or a flex-work getaway far more realistic. Direct service also reduces the need to rent a car for long drives from Cabo, putting the peninsula's wilder side within easy reach.

## Plan the Trip

Combine La Paz with a few nights at a beach town along the corridor, or use it as a quiet base to explore the Sea of Cortez by boat. With the new LAX–La Paz link, Baja California Sur just got noticeably closer.
