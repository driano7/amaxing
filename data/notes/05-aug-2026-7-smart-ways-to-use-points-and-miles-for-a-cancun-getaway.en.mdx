## Fly Smarter, Stay Longer

Cancun has long been one of the most popular beach destinations for U.S. travelers, and with the right point strategy it becomes far more affordable. The Points Guy's rundown highlights how combining airline transfer partners with hotel loyalty programs can dramatically cut the cost of a Mexican getaway.

## Airline Transfer Sweet Spots

The biggest savings come from transferring bank points to airline partners that fly nonstop into Cancun International Airport (CUN). Rather than paying cash for peak-season fares, savvy travelers transfer points and lock in award seats months ahead. The key is flexibility: booking mid-week departures and shoulder-season dates usually exposes the best award availability.

## Stretch Your Hotel Points

Once you land, hotel loyalty programs shine. Many of Cancun's top resorts - from all-inclusive giants to boutique properties in the Hotel Zone - are part of major programs, letting you book award nights that would otherwise cost several hundred dollars. Matching your bank points to a hotel transfer bonus window can push redemptions even further.

## Final Tip for a Cheap Cancun Trip

Booking early, stacking transfer bonuses, and keeping an eye on award-sale announcements are the three habits that separate expensive Cancun trips from budget-friendly ones. Pair those tactics with flexible dates and you can spend less on getting there - and more on the beach.
