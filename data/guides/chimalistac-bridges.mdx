# Chimalistac — Stone Bridges & Canals

Chimalistac means “place of stones”: a 17th-century village that survived intact between Insurgentes and Revolución. It looks like a film set but families live there.

**Story facts:**

- Púlpito Bridge was built without a single nail: only compressed volcanic stone.
- Carmen Church has one tower only: the second was never finished for lack of money in 1840.
- The Magdalena River was tubed in 1940: the water sound you hear is storm drainage, not the river.

**Step-by-step (1.8 km, cobblestone, daytime):**

1. **Paseo del Río / Púlpito Bridge** — start at the stone arch. Touch the cold quarry. 10 min.
2. **Carmen Church & Ex-Convent → San Sebastián Garden** — pink quarry and lone bell tower. Sit on Talavera bench. 15 min.
3. **Amargura St → Mystic Bridge** — second, narrower bridge. See houses with hanging bougainvillea. 15 min.
4. **Exit to Insurgentes / San Ángel** — finish at San Jacinto Plaza (Saturday bazaar) or ice cream in Coyoacán. 20 min.

**Easy context:** Chimalistac was orchards feeding the Carmen convent. Each bridge crossed a canal. Today it’s the only place in CDMX where the 17th century remains inhabited, not musealized.

> **Tip:** Sat 10am, combine with San Ángel Bazaar (10am–6pm) 5 min away.

## How to get there from the Zócalo (safe) — San Ángel is off the main circuit, but worth it

**Reference:** Chimalistac/San Ángel is 11 km south of Zócalo. Not walkable, but a gem outside the hustle.

- **Public transit (daytime, safe):**

  1. **Metro Line 3 (green) Zócalo → Zapata** (20 min), then **Metrobús Line 7 → Altavista** (15 min), get off at Altavista and walk 8 min to Chimalistac via Paseo del Río (cobblestone, calm by day).
  2. Alternative: **Metro Line 2 Zócalo → Viaducto →** Metrobús **Line 7** or `Zapata–San Ángel` bus (ask at the stop, daytime only).

- **Uber/Didi (recommended, safest & direct):** Request **outside Metro Miguel Ángel de Quevedo** (south exit, lit and with shops) to `Plaza San Jacinto, San Ángel`. $120–170 MXN from downtown (20–30 min no traffic). **How to request well:** enter the station, exit south, request there (not on the street), verify model/plates, share trip with your group and share location on WhatsApp. Avoid requesting in Chimalistac alleys.

- **Time:** 30–45 min total. For the return, request Uber **from San Ángel Inn** (well-known, safe) to your hotel.

> **Amaxing tip:** This is how you arrive well, safe, without doubts, and enjoy. Daytime is calm and family-friendly; at night return by Uber. If you go Saturday, combine with **San Ángel Bazaar (10am–6pm)** 5 min away.
