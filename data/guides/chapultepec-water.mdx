# Chapultepec II — Water, Forest & Murals

The Second Section is Chapultepec without crowds: here forest becomes water system and murals are underwater, not in a museum.

**Story facts:**

- Cárcamo de Dolores (1951) pumped Lerma water to the city: Rivera painted inside the tunnel where water flowed.
- Tláloc Fountain (1952) is a 30-meter god lying down: you only get it from the air.
- The Botanical Garden holds 800 desert and forest species right between Castillo and Anthropology and almost nobody enters.

**Step-by-step (3.0 km, forest, bring water):**

1. **Audiorama → Botanical Garden** — enter via Chivatito. Silence and shade. 15 min.
2. **Cárcamo de Dolores / Tláloc Fountain** — ask to turn on the tunnel (sometimes open). See mural from below. 25 min.
3. **Small Lake → Floating Causeway** — cross the elevated bridge through canopy. Volcano views on clear days. 20 min.
4. **Compositores Ave → Constituyentes exit** — close at Los Pinos viewpoint (former presidential residence). 20 min.

**Easy context:** Chapultepec was Tenochtitlan’s aqueduct. Every fountain you see is a layer: Mexica, viceregal, Porfirian, modern.

> **Tip:** Wed or Sun early. Audiorama plays jazz at noon and almost nobody knows.
