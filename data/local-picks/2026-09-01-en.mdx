Tucked behind a restored 1930s facade on República de Guatemala, Colmillo opened in late August and locals are already lining up for its wood-fired espadín tacos and fermented chili salsas. The chef, formerly of Contramar, focuses on hyper-seasonal market ingredients with a tasting menu at $850 MXN. Go at 7pm for the quietest service and order the roasted cauliflower with pipián negro. Why locals recommend it now: it is the first true new opening in the area that feels rooted, not gentrified.
