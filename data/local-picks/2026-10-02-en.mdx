Escape the hustle and bustle in the peaceful Jardín Centenario, a hidden gem in Coyoacán. While many flock to the more famous plazas, this tranquil garden offers a glimpse into authentic local life. Locals gather here to chat, play chess, and simply relax under the shade of the trees. It’s a beautiful spot to enjoy a _marquesita_ (a crispy crepe-like treat) from a nearby vendor. Best visited during the late afternoon when the light is golden and the atmosphere is particularly charming. Easy to reach by Metro (Coyoacán station).
