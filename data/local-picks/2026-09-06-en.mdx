Running until October 15, this exhibition traces corn as material, myth and politics through contemporary installations and archival photos. The central room has a living milpa you can walk through, scented with epazote. Go on Tuesday morning for the quietest visit. Why locals recommend it: it connects food, history and art in one walk.
