The queue moves fast because pulque is already poured.

**Why go:** The cleanest, friendliest pulquería for first-timers. No pulque knowledge needed.

**What to order:** Guava cured (September), natural with salt and lime, and pata tostadas. A liter is for two.

**Logistics:** Mon–Sun 2pm–2am, cash and card. Live music Thu–Sat 9pm.

> **Tip:** First time? Order “campechano” (half natural, half cured).
