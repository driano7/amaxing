# Local Picks - September 2026

Monthly guide for visitors staying 2-7 days. Curated by local editors, avoiding the generic.

## 1. La Esquina del Arte

**Roma · \$\$** · _coffee, art, brunch, new opening_

La Esquina del Arte is the hottest new spot in Roma, blending a cozy coffee shop with a rotating art gallery. Locals are loving the relaxed vibe, perfect for a mid-morning coffee and pastry or a leisurely weekend brunch. The gallery showcases emerging local artists, with new exhibitions every month. Try their signature avocado toast with smoked salmon – it’s a local favorite! It’s a bit tricky to find tucked away on a side street, but the art and atmosphere are worth the hunt. Best to go before 11am on weekends.

## 2. Jardín Centenario

**Coyoacan · \$** · _neighborhood, gardens, history, local life_

Escape the hustle and bustle in the peaceful Jardín Centenario, a hidden gem in Coyoacán. While many flock to the more famous plazas, this tranquil garden offers a glimpse into authentic local life. Locals gather here to chat, play chess, and simply relax under the shade of the trees. It’s a beautiful spot to enjoy a _marquesita_ (a crispy crepe-like treat) from a nearby vendor. Best visited during the late afternoon when the light is golden and the atmosphere is particularly charming. Easy to reach by Metro (Coyoacán station).

## 3. Noches de Jazz en El Jazz Club

**Juarez · \$\$\$** · _jazz, nightlife, music, cultural event_

El Jazz Club in Juarez is hosting "Noches de Jazz," a monthly series featuring both renowned Mexican jazz musicians and international guests. Locals are buzzing about the quality of the performances and the intimate atmosphere. It's a sophisticated night out with expertly crafted cocktails and a vibrant energy. The venue is a little pricey, but the experience is worth it. Check their website for the October lineup – usually announced a week beforehand. Arrive early, especially on weekends, as it fills up quickly. Reservations recommended.
