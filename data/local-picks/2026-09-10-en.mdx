At 5:15pm sun through the yellow glass paints the stair pink.

**Why go:** It’s the only Barragán house kept as he left it. Every wall is a color study.

**How to book:** Guided visits of 8 only, reserve via the official Foundation site 2 weeks ahead. No resale.

**Logistics:** Tue–Sun 10:30am–5:30pm, closed Mondays. No flash, yes to photos. Wear socks.

> **Tip:** Tue or Wed, fewer schools and cleaner light.
