Arrive 7:30am via López entrance. The first aisle smells of sweet bread and broth.

**Why go:** Family stalls have been in the same spot for 40 years. No English menu, just what came out of the pot.

**What to order:** Bone-marrow sope with green salsa, chicken broth with hierba santa and clay-pot coffee with piloncillo. To take away, the cheese stall at the corner vacuum-packs.

**Logistics:** Mon–Sat 7am–6pm, closed Sundays. Cash and transfers. Sit at the counter, not at tables.

> **Tip:** Ask “what came out today?” and let the cook choose.
