Last Wednesday of September, 28 museums stay open until 10pm with free entry, live music and special tours. The Bellas Artes night tour with the lights off is the highlight, but locals head to the smaller spots like Museo del Estanquillo for the rooftop screening. Tip: start at 7pm at MUNAL and walk south; the streets are closed to traffic and filled with performers.
