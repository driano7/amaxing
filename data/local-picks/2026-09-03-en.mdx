Above the new Casa Prim building, Noche Alta has a low-lit terrace with a clear view of the Torre Latinoamericana. The cocktail list leans on Mexican distillates: try the Sotol Sour or the Hoja Santa Negroni. DJ sets start at 10pm Thursday to Saturday, but arrive at 8:30pm for golden hour and an empty bar. Why locals love it: it feels intimate, not scene-y, and the staff actually remembers your name.
