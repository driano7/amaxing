On Liverpool 88, Dosis is a tiny, light-filled spot where the baristas weigh every shot and the vinyl collection never stops. Try the cold brew with piloncillo or the flat white with Oaxacan beans from Pluma Hidalgo. The concha with brown butter is baked fresh at 8am and sells out by 11. Why now: it has become the morning office for freelancers in Juárez and a calm alternative to the busier Roma spots.
