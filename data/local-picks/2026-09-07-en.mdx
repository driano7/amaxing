Between Condesa and Centro, Escandón is having a moment without the hype. Walk from Parque Morelos to the new natural wine bar Vinos Tontos and the bookstore Casa Bosques Sur. Saturday morning, the tiny market on Progreso sells sourdough and Oaxacan cheese. Why now: it is what Roma was five years ago — walkable, lived-in, and still affordable for a long lunch.
