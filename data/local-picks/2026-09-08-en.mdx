September is still rainy season, which means the canals are at their greenest. Join the 6pm night paddle from Cuemanco: you row through chinampas as the sun sets, then stop at a chinampa for pulque curado and quesadillas made on the spot. Groups of 8, headlamps provided. Why locals recommend it: it is cooler and emptier than the daytime party boats.
