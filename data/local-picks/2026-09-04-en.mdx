Not the main museum, but the small project room inside a Polanco townhouse where Casa Wabi quietly shows one artist at a time. This month: a textile installation by a Mixtec collective, with looms you can touch. Only 12 people per hour, book via DM. Why locals recommend it: it is the opposite of the big-ticket museums — quiet, tactile, and you will be the only tourist there.
