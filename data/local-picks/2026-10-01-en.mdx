La Esquina del Arte is the hottest new spot in Roma, blending a cozy coffee shop with a rotating art gallery. Locals are loving the relaxed vibe, perfect for a mid-morning coffee and pastry or a leisurely weekend brunch. The gallery showcases emerging local artists, with new exhibitions every month. Try their signature avocado toast with smoked salmon – it’s a local favorite! It’s a bit tricky to find tucked away on a side street, but the art and atmosphere are worth the hunt. Best to go before 11am on weekends.
