# Tourist Police in Mexico City (2026 Update)

In the most touristic areas of Mexico City you can find **Tourist Police** officers who are trained to help visitors with confidence. They are part of the Secretaría de Seguridad Ciudadana (SSC) and stand out because they speak **multiple languages**:

- English, French, Italian
- _Nahuatl_ and _Totonaca_ (native languages of Mexico)
- Some officers also speak German, Japanese and even Mandarin — ask and they will radio a colleague.

They wear a **sand-colored uniform** (boots, pants, bulletproof vest) and a **light blue shirt** with a `Tourist Police` patch. All have training in assistance, protection and first response for tourists.

## Where you will find them (2026)

1. **Centro Histórico** — around Zócalo, Palacio de Bellas Artes and Alameda Central
2. **Paseo de la Reforma** — Angel of Independence and Monumento a la Revolución
3. **Coyoacán** — Jardín Centenario, Museo Frida Kahlo and Museo Diego Rivera Anahuacalli
4. **Xochimilco** — Nativitas and Cuemanco piers
5. **Polanco** — Lincoln Park, Avenida Presidente Masaryk and Chapultepec Park entrances

They work **24/7 in shifts** and are very approachable — just say `Turist Police, help?` and they will assist.

## What they told us at the Frida Kahlo Museum

- **Who is a tourist?** Anyone from outside Mexico City, even from the neighboring State of Mexico. You are entitled to assistance.
- **Languages:** Everyone speaks Spanish + English; the squad as a whole covers 6+ languages via radio.
- **If you are overcharged or robbed:** If an agency, hotel, airline or business overcharges you, or if your belongings are stolen, they will **open a report, accompany you to the Ministerio Público if needed, and follow up until you leave the city/country**.
- **App "Mi Policía" (2026):** The official SSC app [Mi Policía](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.moobky.MyPolice) has an emergency button (camera-like icon). It shares your GPS with the nearest unit. It also links to a **WhatsApp assistance line +52 55 4891 1116** — save it. Click [here](https://wa.me/525548911116) to open WhatsApp directly.
  <div>![Mi Policía App](/static/images/miPolicia.jpeg)</div>

> **Tip:** Save `+52 55 4891 1116` in your contacts as `Tourist Police CDMX` before you go out. If you need an interpreter, say the language and they will patch one in.
