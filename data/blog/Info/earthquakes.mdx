# Mexico is a seismic country — be prepared, not scared

Mexico sits on the collision of the Cocos, Pacific and North American plates. Big quakes like [1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Mexico_City_earthquake), [2017-09-19](https://edition.cnn.com/2017/09/19/americas/mexico-earthquake/index.html) and [2022-09-19](https://weather.com/news/news/2022-09-22-mexico-earthquake-aftershock-mexico-city-michoacan) are remembered, but **most tremors are mild** and CDMX has a citywide **Seismic Alert (Alerta Sísmica)** via speakers, phones and the free app **SkyAlert** (install it).

Follow the **official 2026 guidance** from CENAPRED / Protección Civil:

## Before

1. Identify objects that could fall (shelves, mirrors, lamps) and secure them.
2. Locate safe zones in your Airbnb/hotel: near structural columns, under sturdy tables.
3. Keep always at hand: **first-aid kit, flashlight, battery radio, power bank, water, copies of passport** (photo on phone).
4. Agree on a meeting point outside the building with your group.
5. Learn the nearest **safe exit** — do not block it with luggage.

## During

1. **Stay calm, do not run** — go to the safe zone you identified.
2. Protect children, elderly and people with disabilities first.
3. Stay away from windows, glass, exterior doors and façades.
4. On the street: stay away from poles, cables and façades that can shed debris.
5. In a building: **do not use elevators**; stay near a column, away from windows.
6. In a crowded place (cinema, market): walk, do not run, toward evacuation doors.
7. Stay away from shelves with falling objects. Drop, cover and hold on.
8. If driving: stop as quickly as possible and stay inside the vehicle.
9. If you are above the 4th floor, **do not try to go down** while shaking — shelter in place.

> **If the Seismic Alert sounds:** you have 30–90 seconds before the wave arrives in CDMX. Do not run downstairs — protect yourself where you are.

## After

1. Wait **10–20 minutes** before re-entering your home/workplace — aftershocks.
2. Inspect for damage; if you smell gas, **turn off gas and power** and open windows.
3. If power is out, use battery flashlights — **no candles, no matches, no smoking indoors**.
4. Cooperate with authorities and have [emergency numbers](/blog/Info/phones) at hand.
5. Be supportive — stay off the phone to keep lines free, use WhatsApp/text.

## For visitors (practical)

- **Ask your hotel/Airbnb:** `¿Dónde es la zona segura aquí?` and `¿Dónde suena la alerta?`
- **Keep shoes by the bed** (glass), and a flashlight.
- **SkyAlert** and **MyShake** notify seconds before you feel it.
