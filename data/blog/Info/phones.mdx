# Important telephones you should know (2026)

Save these in your phone **before** you go out. All work 24/7, free from any phone in Mexico:

1. **Tourist Assistance (CAT / Tourist Police WhatsApp):** **+52 55 4891 1116** — bilingual help, share your location via WhatsApp [wa.me/525548911116](https://wa.me/525548911116)
2. **Locatel Mexico City:** `*0311` or `55 5658 1111` — information, lost objects, family contact
3. **Emergency (Mexico & CDMX):** **911** — police, fire, ambulance (the single number for all)
4. **Civil Protection CDMX:** `55 5683 2222` — risks, flooding, fallen trees
5. **Anonymous Crime Report (Denuncia Anónima):** `089`
6. **Report Police Abuse (Asuntos Internos SSC):** `55 5208 7445`
7. **Embassy assistance (U.S. example):** `55 5080 2000` — for other nationalities, check your embassy in CDMX

## Tips

- **From a Mexican cell:** just dial `911` — no prefix needed. From foreign SIM, dial `+52` + number if WhatsApp.
- **WhatsApp Tourist Police** is often fastest in the Center/Roma/Condesa — they answer in English and can send a unit with GPS.
- **Locatel** can locate hospitals and also help if you are separated from your group — have your meeting point written down.
- **Save offline:** screenshot this list or print it (Ctrl+P).
