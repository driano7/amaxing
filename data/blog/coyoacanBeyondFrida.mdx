# Coyoacán Beyond the Blue House

The Casa Azul line wraps around the block by 11 AM. Most visitors do the museum, buy a magnet, and leave. That's half a neighborhood — arguably the weaker half.

## The order that actually works

**1. The Rivera Anahuacalli studio first.** It's a 10-minute ride away, it opens with a fraction of the crowds, and it reframes everything: Diego wasn't just Frida's husband, he was building a stone temple to house 50,000 pre-Hispanic pieces. Go here first and the Blue House hits differently after.

**2. The market, not the plaza restaurants.** The Coyoacán market's tostadas and quesadillas beat anything on the plaza-facing terraces at half the price. Eat standing up like everyone else.

**3. The Blue House with timed entry.** Book the earliest slot you can. The rooms are small; at noon they're a slow-moving queue with paintings on the walls.

**4. The plaza last.** Coyote fountain, church, churros. This is the dessert, not the meal.

## The thing nobody tells you

Frida and Diego's story makes no sense without the revolution that shaped them and the neighborhood that hosted them. A guide who connects those three things turns five hours of walking into one coherent story instead of three disconnected stops.
